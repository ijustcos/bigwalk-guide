## Reduce motion sickness first

Open **Settings → General** and test the Motion Reduction Overlays. They add a stable visual reference intended to make first-person movement feel more grounded.

On Windows or Mac, also open **Settings → Graphics** and increase the field of view. A wider FOV can reduce the closed-in feeling that contributes to discomfort. Change one option at a time and stop playing if symptoms continue.

## Accessibility features listed by House House

- Skip any challenge
- Rebind every control
- Choose among multiple crosshair styles
- Select hold or toggle behavior for some controls
- Adjust FOV on PC and Mac
- Use a Motion Reduction Overlay
- Carry another character when necessary

The host enables challenge skipping through the accessibility menu when beginning a session. This covers challenges involving hearing, reading, speech, typing, reaction speed or fine motor control.

## Can you play without a microphone?

Yes. In-game text chat follows the same distance, barrier and walkie-talkie rules as voice chat, so it preserves the puzzle design. Avoid using unrestricted external text or voice channels if you want the intended solutions to make sense.
