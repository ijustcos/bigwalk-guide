## The useful answer

Big Walk supports rebinding. Change one action at a time, avoid duplicate assignments and keep point, interact, kick, crouch and communication actions easy to reach.

## Step by step

1. Open Controls in settings.
2. Select an action and press the new input.
3. Resolve duplicate warnings.
4. Test movement and interaction in a safe area.

## Why players get stuck here

Communication puzzles are much harder if pointing or gesture controls are placed on awkward combinations.

## Bind the communication actions first

Movement alone is not enough for Big Walk. Test interact, grab, kick, crouch, point, gesture and any push-to-talk or text control before entering a timed puzzle. Keep actions used together on different fingers and resolve duplicate assignments immediately.

Accessibility settings also offer hold/toggle behavior and different crosshairs. If one player cannot perform a required challenge input, the host can enable challenge skipping when creating the session.

## Verify it before moving on

Change one variable at a time, then retest from a fresh session. That makes it clear which fix worked and avoids turning a connection, audio or input problem into several overlapping problems.
