> **Major progression spoilers below.** Finish the first ending before revealing the full route if you want the intended discovery.

## First ending and Big Goodbye

<Spoiler label="Show first-ending route">
  Complete the colored progression, finish Black Tower and take the Black key to the wall. Pass
  beyond the wall, travel to the large Black Sphere and clear its final cooperative sequence. This
  triggers the first ending and Big Goodbye.
</Spoiler>

## Complete ending and Big Game

<Spoiler label="Reveal the 100% requirements">
  Continue the same save after the first ending. Use the Map Room to clear every unfinished normal
  challenge, then complete all seven purple post-game challenges. Return their rewards, collect the
  final key and take it to the hidden room near the starting area, associated with the broken black
  sphere on the wall. Entering with the completed key triggers the final sequence and Big Game.
</Spoiler>

## Cleanup order

1. Check the large Map Room map for unfinished or unsubmitted normal rewards.
2. Recover loose progression objects before starting long purple routes.
3. Complete the seven purple challenges as a dedicated group.
4. Confirm no normal challenge remains before assuming the final key is bugged.
5. Return to the starting region only after the final key is ready.

The purple rewards are explained separately in [what the purple things do](/guides/purple-gourds).
