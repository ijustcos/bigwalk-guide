## Carry and communication tools

| Tool          | What it does                                    | Carry it when…                                               |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Walkie-talkie | Carries voice or text over long distances       | The group splits for a remote button or scouting route       |
| Waist bag     | Adds a small-item slot                          | You want communication gear without losing your main hand    |
| Backpack      | Adds a full item slot                           | Someone is responsible for keys, rewards or larger equipment |
| Flare gun     | Gives separated players a visible rescue signal | A scout may get lost                                         |
| Paintbrush    | Leaves marks and simple visual notes            | The group needs a trail or shared shorthand                  |
| Laser pointer | Points to a distant visible target              | Nearby players need silent, precise direction                |

## Navigation and scouting

- **Portable map:** shows your position and helps interpret coordinates.
- **Large Map Room map:** tracks challenge and reward status but cannot be carried.
- **Compass:** points north for reliable verbal directions.
- **Binoculars:** inspect distant landmarks before committing to a long route.
- **Telescope:** a fixed scouting device that can reveal or activate distant puzzle information.
- **GPS:** shows precise coordinates and can be attached to a player's backpack.

## Light and sound

A portable lamp makes night travel safer and easier to coordinate. Radios, megaphones and sound devices are not decorative: Big Walk uses sound direction, distance and transmission as puzzle rules. Test an unfamiliar item before abandoning it.

## Inventory strategy

Do not give every player the same loadout. A small party usually benefits from one navigator, one progression-object carrier and redundant long-range communication. Leave a spare hand available because many challenges introduce a physical object midway through the solution.
