## Full checklist

| Achievement  | Requirement                | Best time to do it                  |
| ------------ | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Big Walk     | Cross the drawbridge       | Opening progression                 |
| Big View     | Visit the map              | After Red Tower                     |
| Big Sit      | Ride the chairlift         | After Green Tower                   |
| Big Ride     | Ride the train             | After Blue Tower                    |
| Big Tunnel   | Enter a big tunnel         | After Yellow Tower                  |
| Big Wall     | Go beyond the wall         | Late game                           |
| Big Pack     | Wear a backpack            | Whenever one is found               |
| Big Help     | Wear something on your hip | Whenever a waist item is found      |
| Big Climb    | Reach the highest point    | Exploration cleanup                 |
| Big Makeover | Get shiny                  | Tunnel-side exploration             |
| Big Goodbye  | Finish the game            | First ending                        |
| Big Game     | Completely finish the game | Every challenge and complete ending |

PS5 also awards **Big Trophy** after all other trophies are earned.

## Efficient 100% route

Do not leave the active tower route for every side achievement. Big Walk, Big View, Big Sit, Big Ride, Big Tunnel, Big Wall and Big Goodbye naturally confirm major progression. Collect wearables when convenient, then handle Big Climb, Big Makeover and unfinished challenges during the cleanup before Big Game.

## Hidden achievement spoilers

Big Goodbye is the first ending. Big Game requires every normal challenge, the seven purple post-game challenges and the final ending route. See [endings and 100%](/guides/endings-and-100-percent) for the full sequence.
