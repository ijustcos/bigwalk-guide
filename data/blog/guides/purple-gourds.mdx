## What are they?

Players use many names for the collectible objects: gourds, nubbins, eggs, beans, statues and even kumquats. The purple versions are tied to the optional puzzles that open after the main path.

<Spoiler label="Do they go in a colored tower?">
  Do not assume they work exactly like the red progression rewards. Players have been able to
  complete the Black Tower without placing every purple object into a colored tower. Treat them as
  completion collectibles for the post-game route.
</Spoiler>

## Are they required for the ending?

They are not required for the basic main-game completion. Collect them when aiming for the additional purple challenges and full completion.

## A practical cleanup plan

Finish the normal tower route first, then return to the Map Room and compare its remaining markers with your group’s notes. Give every purple reward to one named carrier, bank it before starting the next challenge, and mark the corresponding puzzle complete. This prevents a group from solving the right puzzle but forgetting where the physical reward was left.
