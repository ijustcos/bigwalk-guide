## Step by step

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a glass room, a semicircle of stones and five outside buttons.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. The locked-in player points toward the outside button matching the lit stone. The outside player presses it; repeat until the progress display fills.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.
