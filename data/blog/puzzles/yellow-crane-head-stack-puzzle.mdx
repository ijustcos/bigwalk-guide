Stack one player on another player’s head, walk under the crane and have the upper player press the underside button. The reward drops from the crane.

## Step by step

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a yellow crane with a red button underneath.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Stack one player on another player’s head, walk under the crane and have the upper player press the underside button. The reward drops from the crane.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.
