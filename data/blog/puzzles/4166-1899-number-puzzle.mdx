## How to identify it

You are in the right place if you see a nubbin inside frosted glass and a hanging plaque showing **4166** and **1899**. The important clue is that the release button is not beside the prize.

<Spoiler label="Show hint 1">
  Treat the two numbers as information that describes a place, not as a code to enter at the case.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Show hint 2">
  Unlocking the Red Tower map room makes the clue much easier to read. Use the large map and
  handheld navigation tools to locate the corresponding point.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Show hint 3">
  Read 4166 and 1899 as coordinates: roughly 41.66 and 18.99. The target is southeast of the
  starting hub, close to the eastern edge of that map grid.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Reveal the solution">
  Open the handheld map and find where the 41 longitude line meets the 18 latitude line. Search
  about two-thirds down that grid square and almost all the way east. A small button is hidden
  behind a rock. Leave one player at the frosted case, send another to hold that button, then take
  the nubbin while the glass is open. The GPS below Green Tower is an alternative: move until its
  readout is approximately 41.66 / 18.99.
</Spoiler>

## Common mistake

Do not send the whole group away from the case. The glass only stays open while the distant button is held, so someone must already be waiting beside the reward.

## What you need

- At least two players
- The map room is strongly recommended
- Walkie-talkies make the final timing much easier

## Exact map-reading method

The large digits give the map lines; the final two digits give the position inside that square.
Treat **66** as roughly two-thirds of the way through the latitude direction, and **99** as almost
the far edge of the longitude direction. The button is deliberately camouflaged behind a rock, so
slow down once the map or GPS says you are close.
