## How to identify it

This guide covers **walls, block stairs, red planks and a timer throw** at **Near Blue Tower — coordinates 3700, 1950**. Confirm the visible objects and coordinates before opening a hint; Big Walk does not display official puzzle names.

## Complete step-by-step solution

<Spoiler label="Reveal every step">

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for walls, block stairs, red planks and a timer throw.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Stack to clear the first wall, hold the door, leapfrog up the steps, crouch under the low wall, synchronize switches and throw the timer to the final holder.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.

</Spoiler>

## Quick checklist

- At least two players are present
- The team has agreed on short signals
- Someone has a free hand or bag slot for the reward
- Send one player ahead of the final throw so the receiver is already beside the holder.

## Verify it before moving on

After the reward appears, choose one carrier and take it to the correct receiver before the group disperses. A solved mechanism and a banked progression item are not always the same thing.
