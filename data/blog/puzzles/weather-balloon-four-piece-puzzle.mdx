## The key idea

The pieces are not an arbitrary picture. Their orientation matches the island and its four colored towers.

<Spoiler label="Show arrangement hint">
  Use the map as your frame of reference. Each piece belongs in the quadrant pointing toward its
  matching colored tower.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Reveal the arrangement">
  Community reports confirm the working order as **Yellow, Green, Red, Blue**, arranged from
  **top-left to bottom-right**.
</Spoiler>

If one piece appears missing, check the platform perimeter and the approach path before resetting the session.
