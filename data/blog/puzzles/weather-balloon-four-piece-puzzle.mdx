## The key idea

The pieces are not an arbitrary picture. Their orientation matches the island and its four colored towers.

## Before moving the pieces

Stand where you can see the whole blue platform and agree which edge of the board counts as north. If two players use different orientations, a correct piece can look wrong and the group will keep undoing its own progress. The large island map is the clearest reference, but the tower colors also work if you already know their directions.

<Spoiler label="Show arrangement hint">
  Use the map as your frame of reference. Each piece belongs in the quadrant pointing toward its
  matching colored tower.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Reveal the arrangement">
  Community reports confirm the working order as **Yellow, Green, Red, Blue**, arranged from
  **top-left to bottom-right**.
</Spoiler>

If one piece appears missing, check the platform perimeter and the approach path before resetting the session.

## How to know the arrangement registered

Place all four pieces before judging the result; a single correct quadrant does not provide a separate confirmation. If the board does not react after the final piece, rotate your viewpoint back to the agreed map orientation, verify the top-left corner first, and then check the remaining colors clockwise. A dropped piece can also land just outside the platform lip.
