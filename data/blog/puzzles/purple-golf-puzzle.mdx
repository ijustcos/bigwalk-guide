## Where is the ball?

The purple version does not give you a new ball. It uses the **same heavy ball from the original puzzle near Red Tower**.

<Spoiler label="Show route hint">
  Get the ball uphill and use the train route toward the area south of Yellow Tower. From there,
  work toward the chairlifts and the final tunnel approach.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Reveal a community-tested route">
  Carry the ball uphill through the opening between the mountains, then angle right toward the
  chairlifts. Another tested route places the ball on top of a train tunnel, takes the train past
  Green Tower, and continues via the rock stop and chairlifts before walking through the tunnel to
  the hole.
</Spoiler>

## Avoid losing the ball

- Move slowly near cliffs and train edges.
- Keep one player below the ball where possible.
- Do not jump away from a chairlift while carrying it.
- If the route goes wrong, regroup before pushing it further downhill.
