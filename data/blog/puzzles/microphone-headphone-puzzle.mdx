## What the puzzle wants

The headset is playing music that matches one of the radio stations around the island. This is a matching and coordination puzzle, not a microphone-volume test.

<Spoiler label="Show hint 1">
  Bring a portable radio to the chair. Sit down and listen carefully to the track in the headset.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Show hint 2">
  Cycle through the radio channels you have unlocked until you hear the same music. Each channel
  corresponds to a station and reward box elsewhere.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Reveal the full solution">
  Identify the matching station, then split up. One player waits beside that station's box while the
  other returns to the chair and holds the button. The distant box opens while the button is held,
  allowing the waiting player to collect the purple gourd.
</Spoiler>

## If you cannot find the matching channel

You may not have unlocked every radio station yet. Stations are identifiable on the large map after opening the Red Tower map room. Continue discovering stations, then repeat the comparison.

## Fast two-player role split

Let the better navigator carry the portable radio and travel to the matching station. The other player stays at the chair so the activation step does not require another full return trip. Confirm both players are at their final positions before anyone holds the button.

If the remote box does not open, compare the chair music and radio track again at the same volume. Similar rhythms can lead the team to the wrong station even when the coordination step is correct.
