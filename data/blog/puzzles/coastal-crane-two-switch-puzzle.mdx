## The useful answer

Put one player at the north switch and one at the south switch, then press them almost together after a visible signal.

## Step by step

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a huge coastal crane and two distant switches.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Put one player at the north switch and one at the south switch, then press them almost together after a visible signal.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.

## Why players get stuck here

Big Walk does not give most challenges official names. Players often search by the visible object, nearby tower or coordinates. This page uses all three so you can confirm the correct puzzle before revealing the answer. A raised lantern is easier to see than a small arm wave, especially at night.

## Quick checklist

- At least two players are present
- The team has agreed on short signals
- Someone has a free hand or bag slot for the reward
- A raised lantern is easier to see than a small arm wave, especially at night.

## Verify it before moving on

After the reward appears, choose one carrier and take it to the correct receiver before the group disperses. A solved mechanism and a banked progression item are not always the same thing.
