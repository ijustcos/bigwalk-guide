## How to identify it

This guide covers **a red house with paired yellow turnstiles** at **Near Blue Tower — coordinates 3505, 1960**. Confirm the visible objects and coordinates before opening a hint; Big Walk does not display official puzzle names.

## Complete step-by-step solution

<Spoiler label="Reveal every step">

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a red house with paired yellow turnstiles.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Enter separate sides. The right player describes five symbols; the left player places matching pieces in order and presses the check button.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.

</Spoiler>

## Quick checklist

- At least two players are present
- The team has agreed on short signals
- Someone has a free hand or bag slot for the reward
- Pick up dropped gear after leaving the turnstiles and use the rear canvas gap or radios if the wall blocks speech.

## Verify it before moving on

After the reward appears, choose one carrier and take it to the correct receiver before the group disperses. A solved mechanism and a banked progression item are not always the same thing.
