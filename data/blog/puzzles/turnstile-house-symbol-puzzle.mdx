## Step by step

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a red house with paired yellow turnstiles.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Enter separate sides. The right player describes five symbols; the left player places matching pieces in order and presses the check button.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.
