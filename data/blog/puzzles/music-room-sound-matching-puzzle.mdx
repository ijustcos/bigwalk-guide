## How to identify it

This guide covers **two sets of sound booths and movable speakers** at **Near Yellow Tower — coordinates 3200, 1260**. Confirm the visible objects and coordinates before opening a hint; Big Walk does not display official puzzle names.

## Hint ladder

<Spoiler label="Show a light hint">
  Agree on repeatable sound words such as rhythm, pitch and pulse instead of only humming louder.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Show a stronger hint">
  Split the team into clear roles before starting: one side observes, holds or signals while the
  other side moves, places or completes the interaction.
</Spoiler>

## Complete step-by-step solution

<Spoiler label="Reveal every step">

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for two sets of sound booths and movable speakers.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. The locked-in player listens to each reference sound and recreates it over the intercom. The outside player finds the matching speaker and hangs it in the correct booth.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.

</Spoiler>

## Quick checklist

- At least two players are present
- The team has agreed on short signals
- Someone has a free hand or bag slot for the reward
- Agree on repeatable sound words such as rhythm, pitch and pulse instead of only humming louder.

## Verify it before moving on

After the reward appears, choose one carrier and take it to the correct receiver before the group disperses. A solved mechanism and a banked progression item are not always the same thing.
