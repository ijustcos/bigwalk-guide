## Step by step

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a hoop and a score meter in the forest.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Throw lanterns through the hoop until the meter fills. Two players can shoot in turn and retrieve misses faster.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.
