## How to identify it

This guide covers **a moon-shaped chair carrying players between symbol boards** at **Near Yellow Tower — coordinates 3570, 1600**. Confirm the visible objects and coordinates before opening a hint; Big Walk does not display official puzzle names.

## Hint ladder

  Backpacks let two players carry four pieces per trip; leave inventory slots free first.
</Spoiler>

<Spoiler label="Show a stronger hint">

## Complete step-by-step solution

<Spoiler label="Reveal every step">

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a moon-shaped chair carrying players between symbol boards.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Read the five-symbol order on the far platform, ferry the matching pieces across on the chair and place them beside the screen before checking.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.

</Spoiler>

## Quick checklist

- At least two players are present
- The team has agreed on short signals
- Someone has a free hand or bag slot for the reward
- Backpacks let two players carry four pieces per trip; leave inventory slots free first.

## Verify it before moving on

After the reward appears, choose one carrier and take it to the correct receiver before the group disperses. A solved mechanism and a banked progression item are not always the same thing.
