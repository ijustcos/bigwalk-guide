## Step by step

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a moon-shaped chair carrying players between symbol boards.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Read the five-symbol order on the far platform, ferry the matching pieces across on the chair and place them beside the screen before checking.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.
