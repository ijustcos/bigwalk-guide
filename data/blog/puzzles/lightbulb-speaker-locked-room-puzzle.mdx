## How to identify it

This guide covers **two locked rooms with incomplete displays** at **Near Green Tower — coordinates 3825, 1930**. Confirm the visible objects and coordinates before opening a hint; Big Walk does not display official puzzle names.

## Complete step-by-step solution

<Spoiler label="Reveal every step">

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for two locked rooms with incomplete displays.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Each player can see the symbols missing from the other display. Use the tannoy in one room and counted red-light flashes in the other to exchange piece numbers.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.

</Spoiler>

## Quick checklist

- At least two players are present
- The team has agreed on short signals
- Someone has a free hand or bag slot for the reward
- Number the nine pieces before locking in; one flash system is far clearer than inventing descriptions mid-puzzle.

## Verify it before moving on

After the reward appears, choose one carrier and take it to the correct receiver before the group disperses. A solved mechanism and a banked progression item are not always the same thing.
