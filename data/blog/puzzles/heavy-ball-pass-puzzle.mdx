## Step by step

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a heavy ball that freezes the carrier in place.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. Stand in front of the carrier and take the ball, then alternate handoffs until the ball reaches the upper goal platform.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.
