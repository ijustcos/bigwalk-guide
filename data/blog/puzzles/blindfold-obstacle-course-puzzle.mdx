## How to identify it

This guide covers **a golden head beside an obstacle course** at **Near Blue Tower — coordinates 3360, 1930**. Confirm the visible objects and coordinates before opening a hint; Big Walk does not display official puzzle names.

## Complete step-by-step solution

<Spoiler label="Reveal every step">

1. Confirm the puzzle by looking for a golden head beside an obstacle course.
2. Place each player in position before activating any timer, lock or moving part.
3. One player wears the golden head and enters with blurred sight. A guide outside moves along the wall and gives short instructions until the wearer reaches the final red button.
4. Collect the red control-panel piece after the confetti and carry it to the tower you are progressing.

</Spoiler>

## Quick checklist

- At least two players are present
- The team has agreed on short signals
- Someone has a free hand or bag slot for the reward
- Use “forward, stop, right, jump, crouch” instead of angles or long sentences.

## Verify it before moving on

After the reward appears, choose one carrier and take it to the correct receiver before the group disperses. A solved mechanism and a banked progression item are not always the same thing.
