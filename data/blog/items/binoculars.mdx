## The useful answer

Find it on top of control-panel towers and beside some radio points. Its main use is to identify distant landmarks, players, lights and puzzle targets.

## Step by step

1. Go to the item location: on top of control-panel towers and beside some radio points.
2. Make sure one player has a free hand, back slot or hip slot before picking it up.
3. Use it to identify distant landmarks, players, lights and puzzle targets.
4. Return it to a memorable landmark if your group does not want to carry it.

## Why players get stuck here

Tools are physical objects in Big Walk: they can be dropped, moved, worn or left behind between destinations. Pair binoculars with a laser pointer or radio so the observer can direct the rest of the team. A useful item is only useful if the rest of the group knows who is carrying it.

## Quick checklist

- Confirm the item visually before leaving the area
- Choose one named carrier
- Tell the group where it will be returned
- Pair binoculars with a laser pointer or radio so the observer can direct the rest of the team.

## Verify it before moving on

Test the item near the place where you found it, then note whether it changes with distance, sound, light or another player. Many tools are communication aids rather than keys that directly unlock one door.
