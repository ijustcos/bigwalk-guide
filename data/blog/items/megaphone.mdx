## The useful answer

Find it on yellow cube blocks near Green Tower, Yellow Tower and the Purple Tunnel. Its main use is to amplify a voice across a very large distance.

## Step by step

1. Go to the item location: on yellow cube blocks near Green Tower, Yellow Tower and the Purple Tunnel.
2. Make sure one player has a free hand, back slot or hip slot before picking it up.
3. Use it to amplify a voice across a very large distance.
4. Return it to a memorable landmark if your group does not want to carry it.

## Why players get stuck here

Tools are physical objects in Big Walk: they can be dropped, moved, worn or left behind between destinations. It broadcasts loudly but does not replace the special logic of receivers or sealed communication rooms. A useful item is only useful if the rest of the group knows who is carrying it.
