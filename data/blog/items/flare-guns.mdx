## The useful answer

## Step by step

1. Go to the item location: inside four ring-shaped structures around the map.
2. Make sure one player has a free hand, back slot or hip slot before picking it up.
3. Use it to fire a colored smoke flare with a short cooldown.
4. Return it to a memorable landmark if your group does not want to carry it.
