## The useful answer

Find it outside the blindfold obstacle course near Blue Tower. Its main use is to blind the wearer so they can enter and complete its communication challenge.

## Step by step

1. Go to the item location: outside the blindfold obstacle course near Blue Tower.
2. Make sure one player has a free hand, back slot or hip slot before picking it up.
3. Use it to blind the wearer so they can enter and complete its communication challenge.
4. Return it to a memorable landmark if your group does not want to carry it.
