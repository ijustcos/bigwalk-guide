## The useful answer

Find it below Red Tower, near the red ravine tunnel and on a central mountainside frame. Its main use is to let separated players talk over distance and coordinate remote switches.

## Step by step

1. Go to the item location: below Red Tower, near the red ravine tunnel and on a central mountainside frame.
2. Make sure one player has a free hand, back slot or hip slot before picking it up.
3. Use it to let separated players talk over distance and coordinate remote switches.
4. Return it to a memorable landmark if your group does not want to carry it.

## Why players get stuck here

Tools are physical objects in Big Walk: they can be dropped, moved, worn or left behind between destinations. Carry them in pairs and test the channel before splitting up. A useful item is only useful if the rest of the group knows who is carrying it.

## Quick checklist

- Confirm the item visually before leaving the area
- Choose one named carrier
- Tell the group where it will be returned
- Carry them in pairs and test the channel before splitting up.

## Verify it before moving on

Test the item near the place where you found it, then note whether it changes with distance, sound, light or another player. Many tools are communication aids rather than keys that directly unlock one door.
