## Step by step

1. Go to the item location: in the tutorial area.
2. Make sure one player has a free hand, back slot or hip slot before picking it up.
3. Use it to make a recognizable sound for nearby regrouping.
4. Return it to a memorable landmark if your group does not want to carry it.

## Why players get stuck here

Tools are physical objects in Big Walk: they can be dropped, moved, worn or left behind between destinations. The cowbell is fun but not a replacement for radios when the group has split across the island. A useful item is only useful if the rest of the group knows who is carrying it.
