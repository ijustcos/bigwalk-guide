## The useful answer

Find it at the paint-palette structure around coordinates 3570, 1190. Its main use is to paint a friend’s head, torso and legs using palette colors.

## Step by step

1. Go to the item location: at the paint-palette structure around coordinates 3570, 1190.
2. Make sure one player has a free hand, back slot or hip slot before picking it up.
3. Use it to paint a friend’s head, torso and legs using palette colors.
4. Return it to a memorable landmark if your group does not want to carry it.

## Why players get stuck here

Tools are physical objects in Big Walk: they can be dropped, moved, worn or left behind between destinations. You cannot paint yourself; metallic paint for Big Makeover is found much later. A useful item is only useful if the rest of the group knows who is carrying it.
