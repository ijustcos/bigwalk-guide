## The useful answer

Find it in a red square frame below Green Tower near the chairlift. Its main use is to show exact live coordinates and solve coordinate clues.

## Step by step

1. Go to the item location: in a red square frame below Green Tower near the chairlift.
2. Make sure one player has a free hand, back slot or hip slot before picking it up.
3. Use it to show exact live coordinates and solve coordinate clues.
4. Return it to a memorable landmark if your group does not want to carry it.

## Why players get stuck here

Tools are physical objects in Big Walk: they can be dropped, moved, worn or left behind between destinations. Put the GPS on a teammate’s back so another player can read it while they move. A useful item is only useful if the rest of the group knows who is carrying it.

## Quick checklist

- Confirm the item visually before leaving the area
- Choose one named carrier
- Tell the group where it will be returned
- Put the GPS on a teammate’s back so another player can read it while they move.

## Verify it before moving on

Test the item near the place where you found it, then note whether it changes with distance, sound, light or another player. Many tools are communication aids rather than keys that directly unlock one door.
