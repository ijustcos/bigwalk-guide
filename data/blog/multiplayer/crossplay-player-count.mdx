## How many players can play?

The official range is **two to twelve**. There is no proper single-player campaign; a lone player can enter the world and look around but will quickly reach challenges that require cooperation.

## What does the world-size choice mean?

When hosting, choose a 2-player, 3-player or 4+ version of the world. Some challenges adapt to that selection. Treat it as the minimum number you expect to have available. If four people start but only two reliably return, choosing the 2-player world prevents the continuing group from being blocked.

## What is the best group size?

House House does not name one ideal size. In practice:

| Group | Experience                                                                      |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2     | Focused, intimate and easiest to schedule; every person carries responsibility. |
| 3–4   | A strong balance for puzzle roles, carrying tools and clear communication.      |
| 5–8   | Faster scouting and more flexibility, with a greater need to assign roles.      |
| 9–12  | A deliberately chaotic social walk; coordination becomes the challenge.         |

## Does Big Walk have crossplay?

Yes. Supported platforms can play together through Join Codes. Big Walk is available on Windows and Mac, PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2. The official FAQ says no other platforms are planned at present.

## Is there split-screen or local couch co-op?

Big Walk is designed as online multiplayer. Several challenges depend on players not hearing or seeing the same information, so playing in the same physical room can undermine them. Separate devices and rooms preserve the intended communication rules.
