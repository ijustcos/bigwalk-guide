## The useful answer

Choose the 3-player world when three players are the minimum group expected for every session. More friends may still join up to the 12-player cap.

## Step by step

1. Start from the host’s save.
2. Select the 3-player world.
3. Assign a caller, carrier and runner on separated puzzles.

## Why players get stuck here

World size sets minimum simultaneous puzzle requirements; it is not a hard maximum on who may join later.

## A useful three-player role split

Use one **caller** to translate symbols or numbers, one **runner** to cross distance or operate the remote side, and one **carrier** to protect tools and collect the reward. Rotate those jobs so one person is not permanently left behind.

If one regular player may miss future sessions, a 2-player world is safer. The world choice is the minimum simultaneous group the host can reliably field.

## Verify it before moving on

Before inviting everyone, agree on the host, world size, voice or text preference and the next session time. Those four details prevent most groups from losing progress or creating a world they cannot continue.
