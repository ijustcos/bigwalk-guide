## Step by step

1. List completed towers and remaining challenges.
2. Say whether spoilers are allowed.
3. Choose a host who owns the relevant save.
4. Plan more than one sitting if cleanup is large.

## Why players get stuck here

A fresh host cannot automatically continue another host’s world progress, even if every player earned some personal achievements.

## Quick checklist

- Read the quick answer before changing settings or leaving the area
- Keep the same host when world progress matters
- Match the game version across the group
- Use the related guide for the next step

## Verify it before moving on

Never publish a permanent friend code, account login or other private detail. Share only the information needed to judge compatibility, then exchange the temporary Join Code when the group is ready.
