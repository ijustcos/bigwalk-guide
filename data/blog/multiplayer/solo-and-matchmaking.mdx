## Is there matchmaking?

No. Big Walk does not place you into a lobby with random strangers. A host creates a session and shares its Join Code with the people they want to invite.

## Can you play alone?

You can enter and explore by yourself, but many puzzles depend on separated players, simultaneous buttons or long-distance communication. Solo play is best treated as exploration, not the intended full progression route.

## How to find people

Use our [Find Players](/find-players) board to filter short-lived listings by platform, region, language, microphone preference and experience. No player account is required.

## Who keeps the save?

The host owns the session progress. For a group continuing across multiple sessions, keep the same host whenever possible.
