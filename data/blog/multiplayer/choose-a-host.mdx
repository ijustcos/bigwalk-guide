## The useful answer

Choose the person most likely to attend every future session, with a stable connection and enough time to load the save before everyone joins.

## Step by step

1. Compare schedules before starting.
2. Let the most reliable attendee host.
3. Write down the chosen world size and group goal.

## Why players get stuck here

Progress belongs to the host’s save. Picking by fastest connection alone is a mistake if that person rarely returns.

## Quick checklist

- Read the quick answer before changing settings or leaving the area
- Keep the same host when world progress matters
- Match the game version across the group
- Use the related guide for the next step
