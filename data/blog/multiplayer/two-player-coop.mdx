## The useful answer

Yes. Two players is the minimum supported group, and the host should select the 2-player world so every scaled objective remains completable.

## Step by step

1. Choose the most reliable player as host.
2. Select the 2-player world at session creation.
3. Carry radios and divide puzzle roles clearly.

## Why players get stuck here

A 3-player or 4+ world can ask for more simultaneous targets than a duo can operate, so world size matters more than lobby capacity.

## What changes for a duo

Two-player worlds keep simultaneous requirements completable by two people, but they do not remove the core information splits. Expect one player to observe or hold a control while the other moves, places or reports back. Carry paired walkie-talkies and avoid both players filling their hands with tools before a reward appears.

Extra friends may join later; the 2-player choice controls puzzle configuration, not the lobby maximum.

## Verify it before moving on

Before inviting everyone, agree on the host, world size, voice or text preference and the next session time. Those four details prevent most groups from losing progress or creating a world they cannot continue.
