## Before heading to Yellow

Red Tower's map makes the route easier, and Green Tower's chairlift reduces the climb, but neither is a strict requirement once the relevant areas are reachable. Bring a light because the reward is an underground travel system.

## Unlock sequence

1. Gather five physical rewards from unfinished challenges.
2. Place them in the Yellow receiver.
3. Carry the released key through every Yellow cutter.
4. Use the finished key at the tunnel mechanism.
5. Enter the now-open underground route to confirm the system and unlock **Big Tunnel**.

## Why the tunnels matter later

They provide shortcuts between distant regions and are involved in post-progression exploration, including the route associated with getting shiny. If you are only standing at a tunnel entrance and it remains closed, the Yellow key route is not complete.
