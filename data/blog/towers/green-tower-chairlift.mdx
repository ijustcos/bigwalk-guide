## Why complete Green early?

The chairlift is the most useful early transport upgrade. It creates a high route toward the Blue, Yellow and Black regions and cuts repeated uphill travel.

## Green progression loop

1. Solve five accessible challenges and keep their physical rewards together.
2. Submit them at the Green receiver.
3. Collect the raw key.
4. Follow every Green cutter marker until the key is finished.
5. Use it at the chairlift activation point.
6. Board a moving chair to confirm the unlock and earn **Big Sit**.

## Nearby challenge types to recognize

The Green route commonly introduces a silent tile-and-pegboard room, the 4166 / 1899 coordinate clue, a multi-screen counting challenge, colored stands with safe and reset states, and a separated beach-house number relay. Exact route order is flexible.

Use [the coordinate puzzle's progressive hints](/puzzles/4166-1899-number-puzzle) if that is the one blocking you.
