## The useful answer

The train unlock station is around 3380, 1740, northwest of Blue Tower and tucked into the mountain. Use the Blue Tower key there.

## Step by step

1. Complete Blue Tower.
2. Follow all five cutter arrows down the mountain.
3. Insert the finished key at the station.
4. Board any of the three moving trains.

## Why players get stuck here

The key activates the whole rail loop, not only the red train visible near the station.

## How to confirm the railway is active

After the finished Blue key is inserted, station clocks and arriving carriages make the change obvious. Wait for a moving train and board it to trigger Big Ride; standing at a platform is not enough. The unlock applies to the network rather than one colored carriage.

If nothing arrives, verify that five rewards were submitted at Blue Tower and that the key passed through all five cutters.

## Verify it before moving on

Use a fixed landmark and one set of coordinates when giving directions. If the group splits, leave one player at the landmark while the others search so everyone has a reliable point to return to.
