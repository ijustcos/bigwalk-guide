## The useful answer

Green Tower is around 3930, 1900 beside the main chairlift station at 3950, 1950. A red frame below it holds the GPS.

## Step by step

1. Travel east using the map grid.
2. Look for the chairlift infrastructure.
3. Climb to the tower and check the red GPS frame below.
